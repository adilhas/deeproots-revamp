### The Rooted Legion

Heroes do not fight alone. Together they form **The Rooted Legion** — a vast army sworn to protect the Great Tree.  
The Legion grows through seeding, referrals, and victories. Each player’s contribution is recorded and rewarded.

The Rooted Legion is led by **100 Rootopian Commanders**.  
Each Commander is a symbol, a leader, and a voice in the war for DeWorld.

Together, they declare:  
*“This is Rootopia. The war begins now.”*

### Gameplay Structure

Rootopia includes five immersive systems, each backed by blockchain logic:

1. **Landing Page – Call to Hero**  
   - Entry point into the story and the Legion  
   - Animated video calls players to defend Rootopia  

2. **Seeding Page – Stake to Grow**  
   - Stake tokens for APY and early in-game resources  
   - Seeds represent life force, unlocking weapons and land  

3. **Referral Page – Build Your Army**  
   - Invite warriors to join the Legion  
   - Referrals strengthen both inviter and invitee with boosts  

4. **Battle Page – Five-Level System**  
   - Progress through lore-driven battle levels  
   - Unlock bosses, animation, and rewards at each stage  

5. **Dashboard Page – Stats & Rewards**  
   - View army size, assets staked, rank, and prize history
