# INNOFI  
**Vote & Fund DeWorld** — where community-driven innovation comes to life.

InnoFi.earth is the funding, grant, and governance platform of the Deep Roots ecosystem.  
It empowers users to vote, fund, and directly influence the development of decentralized projects — from idea to launch.

---

## Overview  

InnoFi bridges capital and community through decentralized funding.  
It enables milestone-based grants, open proposal submissions, and real-time governance powered by on-chain wallet connections.

---

## Key Features  

**Community-Driven Innovation**  
Fund and vote on projects that shape the future of DeWorld.

**Milestone-Based Funding**  
Support projects step-by-step — approve or deny fund releases based on verified progress.

**Democratic Approval**  
One wallet, one vote. Project approval is decided by the community.

**Empowering Creators**  
Enable innovators to raise funds, gain exposure, and deliver on their vision.

**Transparency & Control**  
Track contributions, funding status, and voting outcomes — all on-chain.

**Secure Wallet Integration**  
Connect The Address Wallet for secure participation and contribution management.

---

## Ecosystem Integration  

- **The Address Wallet** – Connect, vote, and fund directly from your profile.  
- **Foundation** – Seed and support early-stage applicants through ecosystem incentives.  
- **ShareValue** – Graduate top creators into funded projects via InnoFi.  
- **Saxophone** – Tune into live pitch sessions, proposal reviews, and progress updates.
