DeepRoots Roadmap – Phase 5

Phase 5 marks the beginning of coordinated public rollouts. 
Some tools may appear earlier due to internal development progress, but official release cycles will be aligned with community milestones.

Private Ecosystem Disclosure Protocol
- Alliances, flagship dApp integrations, and strategic partnerships will be revealed exclusively to verified Foundation Members via the private dashboard during closed phases.
- No public disclosure will occur until full activation.
- Access is privilege, not publicity.

Tech Access Note
- Deep Roots will remain in closed development mode until 2027.
- In Year 3, full documentation, SDKs, and dev tools will be opened to empower builders across the ecosystem, after the foundation is fully established, audited, and secured.

Trust, Governance & Security
- 40% of token supply locked with AI-governed mechanics (Seeding + CopyMe)
- DAO Treasury and Voting active from 2026
- Open audits + blockchain explorer
- SDKs for verified builders
- No custody; full user control preserved

Foundation Access

  • Tier 1: Foundation Seat ($5k – $9,999)  
  • Tier 2: Core Foundation Seat ($10k – $99,999)  
  • Tier 3: Founder Seat ($100k – $1M+)
