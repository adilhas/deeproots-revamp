# OCEAN BAZAAR  
**A New Wave of NFT Ownership – Dive Into the Real World of Digital Collectibles**

OceanBazaar.earth is the NFT marketplace of the Deep Roots ecosystem — blending art, rarity, utility, and identity into one immersive ocean-themed experience.  
It’s where NFTs become living value — inside your wallet, wardrobe, game, and governance seat.

---

## Overview  

Ocean Bazaar reimagines how NFTs are used — not just collected, but **played, worn, redeemed, governed, and licensed**.  
From fashion to weapons, land to badges — Ocean Bazaar powers the full spectrum of Web3 identity across DeWorld.

---

## Key Features  

**NFT Marketplace with Ocean Flow UI**  
Buy, sell, and auction digital assets in a stunning ocean-inspired interface.

**Easy Sell & Auction**  
Upload, list, or run timed auctions with secure wallet-based listing tools.

**Wallet-Connected Trading**  
Track ownership, royalties, bids, and trade history via The Address Wallet.

**GameFi-Ready Integration (Rootopia)**  
Use NFTs purchased on Ocean Bazaar as:
- **Weapons** for combat
- **Land** for staking & territory control
- **Outfits & Wearables** for gameplay advantage
- **Name Badges** for leaderboard identity & social clout

**Foundation Seats as NFTs**  
Buy, sell, or play with verified **Foundation Seats** and **Founder Tokens** — NFTs with on-chain governance access and legacy status.

**Real-World Redeemables**  
Convert digital goods into fashion, gear, and lifestyle products via MetaFabric.

**Trademark & Digital IP Gateway**  
Enable brands, designers, and DAOs to protect and monetize NFTs as **verifiable digital trademarks** inside the Deep Roots IP layer.
