# INNOFARM  
**The Web3 Job Marketplace – Work, Hire, Earn, Grow**

InnoFarm.earth is the decentralized job and freelance platform of the Deep Roots ecosystem.  
It connects talent, teams, and tasks across Web3 using smart contracts, AI, and verified identity.

---

## Overview  

InnoFarm redefines how the decentralized world works — from job posting to payment.  
Built on blockchain, it empowers workers, startups, and DAOs with secure hiring, automated matching, and transparent contracts.

---

## Key Features  

**Decentralized Hiring**  
Post jobs or find work in a secure, blockchain-powered talent network.

**AI Job Matching**  
Smart algorithms connect verified talent with relevant open opportunities.

**Secure Escrow Payments**  
Crypto and fiat payments are held in escrow via smart contracts until tasks are complete.

**Verified Web3 Profiles**  
Use wallet identity, on-chain badges, and optional KYC to build trust and reputation.

**Learn & Grow**  
Access integrated courses and certifications from ShareValue to boost skills and earnings.

**DAO & Startup Access**  
Teams can hire, fund, and build through a fully decentralized gig layer.

---

## Ecosystem Integration  

- **The Address Wallet** – Sync wallet for identity, reputation, and payments  
- **ShareValue** – Access education, skill-building, and credentials  
- **Foundation** – Refer and seed job hubs or DAO employment networks  
- **Saxophone** – Discover audio-based job listings, project pitches, and updates
