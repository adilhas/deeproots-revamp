DEEPROOTS is more than a blockchain.

---

# DEEPROOTS Code of Honor
The values that guide everything we create, protect, and grow.

---

## What Guides Us
- **Respect over ego** – We build with humility. We listen more than we speak.  
- **Truth over hype** – We don't chase trends. We commit to real value.  
- **Community over control** – Power belongs in the hands of people, not platforms.  
- **Transparency over perfection** – We'd rather show progress than pretend polish.  
- **Creativity over conformity** – Innovation requires freedom and bold thinking.  
- **Ownership over permission** – Everyone deserves access. Everyone deserves control.  
- **Consistency over chaos** – We keep showing up, even when no one's watching.  

---

## Our Pledge
- We protect user rights.  
- We write open code.  
- We celebrate collaboration.  
- We defend digital freedom.  

> "Honor is not branding. It's behavior."  
It’s how we treat each other — and what we’re willing to defend.
