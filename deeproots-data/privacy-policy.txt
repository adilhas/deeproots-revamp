# We Are the Privacy. We Are the Protection.
At DEEPROOTS, privacy is not a feature — it's a right.

---

# Privacy Policy – Our Commitment to You
We don't just talk about decentralization — we build it into how we collect, store, and protect your data.

---

## What We Don't Do
- We don't track your behavior  
- We don't sell your data  
- We don't store personal information without your consent  
- We don't allow third-party tracking on our core products  

---

## What We Do
- Store only what's needed — and only if you allow it  
- Use encrypted systems across all platforms  
- Offer anonymous or wallet-based access wherever possible  
- Let you manage your own data through **decentralized identity (DID)** tools  
- Use AI responsibly — with transparency, not intrusion  

---

## How Your Data Is Handled
- End-to-end encryption  
- Minimal metadata  
- Optional login across platforms  
- Open-source audits and smart contract transparency  
- Future DID and self-sovereign profile integration  

---

## No Secrets. No Surveillance.
We're building **DeWorld** — a digital space where you're not the product.  
Your privacy is baked into the roots of the platform, not added later.
