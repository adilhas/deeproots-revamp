# We Are the Access, We Are the Fair Cost
In DEEPROOTS, gas fees aren't a barrier — they're a gateway to affordable blockchain access.

## Gas Fees — Powering DeWorld for Everyone
We believe access to the blockchain should be affordable, predictable, and fair.  

That's why our gas fees are fixed at just **0.0002** — no spikes, no surprises, no limits to what you can build or use.

## Why 0.0002 Matters
- **Ultra Low-Cost**  
  Anyone can transact, no matter their wallet size

- **Fair & Transparent**  
  No sudden surges or hidden costs

- **Easy for Builders**  
  Deploy and scale without worrying about fees

- **Frictionless UX**  
  One price, across every smart contract, game, or app

- **Encourages Growth**  
  More users, more actions, more possibilities

## How It Works
- **0.0002** fixed per transaction  
- Applies across the entire DeWorld ecosystem  
- Collected in **dRC**, your native token  
- Fuels smart contracts, staking, NFTs, trading, and more  
- Redirected to support rewards, seeding, and foundation income  

## Built for the Future
While other chains compete over gas mechanics, DEEPROOTS keeps it simple, stable, and user-first.  

You focus on creating. We keep it moving.

### No Burn. No Waste.
In our first stage, there's no token burning — only circulation, utility, and fair redistribution.  

Every gas fee flows back into the ecosystem — powering rewards, projects, and long-term sustainability.
