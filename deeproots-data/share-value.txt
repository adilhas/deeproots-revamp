# SHAREVALUE  
**Learn, Create, Innovate – A DeWorld Education Hub**

ShareValue.earth is the Learn & Earn platform of the Deep Roots ecosystem.  
It empowers global creators, builders, and learners to master decentralized technologies, contribute to DeWorld, and transform ideas into real-world impact.

---

## Overview  

ShareValue is where blockchain education meets creation and collaboration.  
With AI-driven learning modules, decentralized resources, and global community access, it transforms learning into an active force for innovation.

---

## Key Features  

**AI-Powered Learning**  
Personalized courses and guidance using AI tutors and training assistants.

**Decentralized Tech Resources**  
Access curated materials to build on blockchain, smart contracts, and DeWorld tools.

**Learn & Earn**  
Complete challenges, contribute to community projects, and earn tokens and credentials.

**Global Accessibility**  
Open to learners from all backgrounds — no barriers, just opportunity.

**Innovation Hub**  
Launch and join hackathons, sprints, and global collabs for ecosystem growth.

**Empowering Creators**  
From idea to launch: mentorship, support, and funding paths for creators and builders.

**Mentorship & Idea Sharing**  
Lead groups, run workshops, and become a knowledge provider in your field.

**Knowledge Exchange**  
Teach others, share expertise, and grow your value through impact.
