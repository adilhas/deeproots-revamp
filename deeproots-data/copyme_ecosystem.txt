👕 Fashion – Tokenized digital wearables and Web3-native fashion brands  
💱 Exchange – TeleXcoin DEX for token swaps and liquidity across chains  
🎓 Education – Learn-to-earn and AI-enhanced learning systems  
🚀 Fundraising – InnoLaunch platform for compliant token sales and ecosystem fundraising  
🪙 Tokenization – Real-world asset tokenization and utility models  

🖼️ NFTs – Ocean Bazaar marketplace for NFTs with deep metadata and AI curation

📜 Governance – Community-driven protocol governance with delegate voting
