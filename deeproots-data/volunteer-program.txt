# Volunteer Program – Join the Movement
deeproots.earth is built by people like you — visionaries, supporters, and doers.  

We're opening the gates to a **global Volunteer Program** where you can contribute directly to the future of DeWorld.

---

## Who We’re Looking For
We welcome passionate contributors from all backgrounds:

- **Community Moderators** – Telegram, X, Reddit, Discord  
- **Translators** – English ↔ Arabic, French, Spanish, Chinese  
- **Web3 Writers & Content Creators**  
- **Designers, Meme Creators, Motion Artists**  
- **Educators, Event Hosts**  
- **Beta Testers** – Test launches on the DEEPROOTS ecosystem  

> No coding experience required. If you're aligned with the mission, you're qualified.

---

## What You’ll Receive
- Recognition on the official site  
- Priority access to token drops, events, and Foundation invites  
- Monthly **dRC-based reward pool**  
- Private Discord/Telegram volunteer hub  
- Opportunity to grow into official ambassador or ecosystem role  

---

## Ways to Contribute
We welcome contributions in every field, demographic, and niche:

- Moderate our community channels  
- Translate ecosystem updates  
- Create explainer videos, social threads, or memes  
- Help beta test **RootOpia** or **The Address Wallet**  
- Host local DEEPROOTS meetups  
- Share DEEPROOTS at universities or dev hubs  

---

## Apply to Volunteer
We review applications weekly and onboard volunteers into private channels.  
All roles are **remote, flexible, and part of the mission to decentralize the internet**.

> Submissions are reviewed weekly. All data is confidential.
