# Simple, Transparent, and Respectful
**Cookie Policy – DEEPROOTS**  

At DEEPROOTS, we believe in **privacy by design** — not intrusive tracking.  
We use cookies sparingly, only where they improve your experience or help us protect the platform.

---

## What We Use Cookies For
- Remember your preferences (like dark mode or language)  
- Secure your session if you log in  
- Understand site performance (basic analytics, privacy-respecting)  
- Simplify navigation across DeWorld products  

---

## What We Never Do
- No third-party marketing cookies  
- No data resale or behavioral tracking  
- No personalized ads or invasive profiling  

---

## Your Control
You can manage cookies in these ways:
- Accept/decline non-essential cookies anytime  
- Clear or block cookies via browser settings  
- Use the site with only required cookies  

---

## Our Commitment
- Essential cookies only by default  
- Optional cookies require consent  
- All data stays within DEEPROOTS
