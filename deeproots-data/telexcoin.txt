# TELEXCOIN DEX  
Unlock the Future of Decentralized Access.

**Swap → Trade → Seed → Talk → Grow** — the liquidity engine of the Deep Roots ecosystem (DeWorld).  
Telexcoin is the native DEX powering token exchange, liquidity pooling, live podcasting, and Foundation-based seeding — all connected in real time.

---

## Overview  

Telexcoin.earth is the decentralized exchange (DEX) of the Deep Roots ecosystem.  
It allows users to swap tokens, add/remove liquidity, access Foundation seeding, join INNOLAUNCH launches, and engage in live trader podcasts and discussion — all in one place.

---

## Key Features  

**Instant Token Swaps**  
Trade Deep Roots tokens and partner assets instantly.

**Liquidity Pools**  
Earn by providing liquidity for supported trading pairs.

**INNOLAUNCH Integration**  
Join token launches and early-stage projects directly from the DEX.

**Foundation Seeding Access**  
Seed into ecosystem initiatives, refer users, and unlock benefits.

**Live Podcast Hub**  
Built-in Saxophone channel for live AMAs, trader conversations, and ecosystem news.  
Connect directly with traders, founders, and Foundation members to share strategies and experiences.

**Referral Rewards**  
Earn rewards through successful project referrals and seeding campaigns.
