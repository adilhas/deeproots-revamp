# What is Full Seat?

Full Seat is your exclusive position in the **DEEPROOTS Foundation** where early supporters earn from the entire ecosystem.  
It’s not just access. It’s ownership.

## What a Full Seat Gives You
- **Ecosystem Utility Share**  
  Command first access to protocol utilities across the DEEPROOTS ecosystem.  

- **12% Yield – On-Chain Seeding Access**  
  Participation terms defined by smart contracts. Protocol-based annual rate.  

- **Referral Rewards**  
  Earn by inviting others to join the ecosystem.  

- **Private Ecosystem Access**  
  Includes whitelist spots, limited features, and exclusive utility drops.  

- **Governance Power**  
  Shape platform decisions in the future DAO.  

- **RootOpia GameFi Power**  
  Boosted benefits inside the game world.  

## Why Seats Are Limited
Only a fixed number of Full Seats exist — and not every project uses them.  
This keeps access **rare, valuable, and community-first.**

## How to Claim Your Full Seat
1. **Join the Foundation Seeding Round**  
2. **Lock your dRC tokens**  
3. **Get assigned a Full Seat** with benefits tied to your round  

> Early rounds offer higher discounts, better rewards, and priority access.  

## Who It’s For
- Long-term believers in decentralization  
- Early adopters of DEEPROOTS and DeWorld  
- Builders, validators, and supporters who grow the ecosystem
