# INNOLAUNCH  
**Empowering DeWorld Projects on the DeepRoots Blockchain**

InnoLaunch.earth is the official launchpad of the Deep Roots ecosystem, designed to accelerate next-generation Web3 startups.  
It connects vetted teams with structured funding rounds, community support, and on-chain launch mechanisms.

---

## Overview  

InnoLaunch provides a clear multi-phase launch pipeline:  
- **First Round** – internal milestone-based funding via InnoFi  
- **Private Round** – Foundation-based ecosystem access  
- **HNWI Access** – Secure private entry for external high-net-worth contributors  
- **Community Referrals** – DeepRoots Foundation referral rewards  
- **Public Launch** – Token goes live on **Telexcoin DEX**

---

## Key Features  

**Phased Launch Structure**  
From internal grants to global trading — structured, staged, and trackable.

**On-Chain Fundraising**  
Transparent wallet-based contribution system with milestone tracking.

**Foundation Integration**  
Private round access via Foundation credentials and referrals.

**Public Market Access**  
Final stage goes live on **Telexcoin DEX**, with liquidity support and trading pairs.

**Creator Empowerment**  
Support for token design, marketing, legal, and fundraising strategies.

**Multi-Tier Investor Access**  
Open funding lanes for Foundation, HNWI, DAO pools, and the public.
