## Key Features  

**All-in-One Dashboard**  
View tokens, NFTs, Foundation Seats, voting power, and GameFi activity in one clear view.  

**Portfolio Overview**  
Real-time updates track your portfolio growth and net value dynamically.  

**Profile + Wallet Address**  
Manage multiple wallets under one profile. Seamlessly switch between accounts.  

**Send / Receive / Buy / Sell**  
Swap tokens, buy with fiat, and transfer assets with intuitive controls.

Hold and manage assets across supported blockchains with built-in network switching.  

**View & Manage Assets**  
Monitor your crypto, full seats, governance votes, NFTs, and GameFi achievements — all from a single wallet.
