# Our Roadmap

## 2025–2027

### Phase 1 — Core Infrastructure Launch  
*Current phase — The roots are planted, our world begins.*  
**Q3–Q4 2025**  
- DEEPROOTS.Earth goes live — the gateway into DeWorld  
- Socials activated (X, LinkedIn, Telegram)  
- Launch Foundation Seat Funnel — verify early contributors with on-chain badge + app visibility  
- Full audit underway: security, speed, and performance  
- Tokenomics revealed — how $dRC moves, works, and empowers  
- Custom chain launches, powered by **PoZ**, **DRVM**, and **EVM compatibility**  
- Seeding Engine preview: a new way to earn and grow together  
- Cross-chain ready: linking with Ethereum, BSC, and more  

---

### Phase 2 — Activate the Ecosystem Core  
*The users rise. Identity, rewards, and purpose converge.*  
**Q1–Q2 2026**  
- Foundation Seat Campaign continues  
- Referral system drops — invite your circle, earn rewards, expand fast  
- Activate On-Chain Referral Rewards — growth goes viral  
- Deploy **TelexCoin DEX** — swap, trade, and own liquidity  
- Launch **The Address Wallet** — root access into the DeWorld ecosystem  

---

### Phase 3 — Engage & Expand the Movement  
*The ecosystem awakens. Value begins to flow.*  
**Q3–Q4 2026**  
- Launch **RootOpia GameFi** — play, earn, seed, lead  
- Deploy **CopyMe AI Assistant** — your digital mind, mirrored  
- Go live with **Ocean Bazaar** — discover & mint NFTs  
- Finalize Foundation Governance — decentralized by design  
- Launch Mobile Wallet (iOS & Android) — power in your pocket  

---

### Phase 4 — Launch Core Opportunity Platforms  
*Builders, dreamers, and doers — your time begins now.*  
**Q1–Q2 2027**  
- Launch **InnoLaunch** — decentralized Utility Launch engine  
- Launch **InnoFi** — raise funds, distribute grants  
- Launch **GiveFi** — redefine transparent giving  
- Launch **InnoFarm** — connect talent, unlock jobs in Web3  

---

### Phase 5 — Real-World Integration & Creative Expansion  
*The digital root system meets the world. Culture becomes code.*  
**Q3–Q4 2027**  
- Launch **Saxophone** — news, culture, and podcast platform  
- Activate **RootIdentity + DAO Governance** — verified voice & vote  
- Launch **MetaFabric** — own fashion NFTs, print them in real life  
- Launch **DeepAssets** — tokenize real estate, gold, and businesses  
- Launch **ShareValue** — learn, earn, and level up  
- Launch **RootChat** — secure decentralized messaging  
- Release **Dev Tools + SDKs** — builders’ toolkit ready  
- Open Audit Docs & Explorer APIs — transparency unlocked  
- Enable **dApp Builder Onboarding** — your ideas go live  

> Phase 5 reflects coordinated public rollouts. Internal development may lead to earlier release of some tools.

---

## Private Ecosystem Disclosure Protocol 🔒
All strategic partnerships, early ecosystem collaborators, and flagship dApp alignments (developers, producers, artists, studios, script contributors) are disclosed **exclusively to verified Foundation Members.**  

Access is provided via the private Foundation Dashboard during closed contribution phases.  
No public announcements will be made prior to full ecosystem activation.  

---

## Tech Access Note 🔒
DeepRoots will operate in **closed development mode until 2027.**  
In Year 3, we will open full documentation, SDKs, and dev tools to empower builders across the ecosystem once the foundation is fully established, audited, and secured.
