GENERATION 1 – BITCOIN (BTC)
- Launched 2009
- The first decentralized digital currency
- Key innovation: Proof-of-Work (PoW)
- Strength: Store of value, scarcity (21M max supply)
- Limitation: Slow transactions, high fees, limited smart contract ability

GENERATION 2 – ETHEREUM (ETH)
- Launched 2015
- Introduced Smart Contracts and dApps
- Key innovation: Programmable blockchain
- Strength: Ecosystem of DeFi, NFTs, DAOs
- Limitation: High gas fees, scalability issues, centralization risks with validators

GENERATION 3 – DEEP ROOTS (dRC)
- Launched 2025 (3rd-Gen Blockchain + AI)
- Key innovation: Proof-of-Seed (PoZ) + Deep Roots Virtual Machine (DRVM)
- Strengths:
  • 41-year fair distribution of dRC (100M supply, built to last generations)  
  • Integrated AI + Blockchain  
  • 20+ Web3 dApps (finance, jobs, education, governance, media, GameFi)  
  • Fast, low fees, highly scalable  
  • Rootopia, InnoFi, TeleXcoin, Ocean Bazaar, CopyMe AI, and more  
- Vision: Build “DeWorld” – a decentralized internet owned by people
- Limitation: Early stage ecosystem (still expanding globally)

COMPARISON SUMMARY
- BTC = Digital Gold (Gen 1, Store of Value)  
- ETH = Programmable Money (Gen 2, Smart Contracts)  
- dRC = Decentralized World (Gen 3, AI + Blockchain, fair long-term design)
